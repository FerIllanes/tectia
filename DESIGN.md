## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, technical industrial identity tailored for commercial construction, architectural waterproofing, and elastomeric coating solutions. 

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Architects, building contractors, property managers, civil engineers, and specialized coating applicators seeking certified structural protection.
- **Brand Personality:** Resilient, technical, precise, and authoritative. The interface conveys structural invulnerability, certified waterproofing standards, and German/Nordic-grade engineering reliability.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, uncompromised structural security, absolute clarity on technical specifications, and immediate trust in warranty terms.

### Design Style: Precision Industrial Architecture
The system employs a crisp, corporate-industrial aesthetic characterized by:
- Pure geometric discipline derived from architectural elevations and roof envelope cross-sections.
- Sharp micro-surfaces balanced with controlled containment borders (low-contrast cool slate lines).
- High visual cadence prioritizing specification data, application coverage calculators, technical compliance badges, and layer-by-layer membrane anatomy.
- Zero decorative clutter: every visual accent maps directly to system durability, certification status, or procedural hierarchy.

## Colors

The color palette directly extracts and balances the tripartite identity of the brand symbol:

### Core Palette
- **Deep Industrial Blue (`#1B3B6F` - Primary):** Serves as the anchor of stability, waterproofing membrane identity, and corporate reliability. Used for major structural surfaces, navigation banners, authoritative headlines, primary button fills, and header elements.
- **Warm Golden Ochre (`#F4B223` - Secondary / Warranty Accent):** Communicates thermal protection, elite certification, sun/UV resistance, and high-tier performance features. Used for warranty badges (e.g., "Garantía 10 Años"), star ratings, progress indicators, and key metric callouts.
- **Signal Red (`#C9181E` - Tertiary / Action Accent):** Represents high alert, chemical-grade sealing potency, emergency leak repair systems, and decisive conversion elements. Deployed strictly for primary CTAs (e.g., "Solicitar Cotización Técnica", "Descargar Ficha Técnica"), active alert badges, and critical warning tags.
- **Industrial Carbon Neutral (`#242A35` - Neutral Slate):** A deep charcoal-slate engineered for extreme legibility in technical body copy, high-density spec sheets, and neutral UI elements, replacing pure black with architectural slate.

### Supporting Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Cool Off-White/Concrete Mist) maintains a sterile, modern architectural foundation that keeps contrast high without harsh glare.
- **Surface Containers:** `#FFFFFF` pure white cards with crisp `#E2E8F0` borders replicate structural architectural blueprints and technical data sheets.
- **Success / Barrier Intact:** `#15803D` for test approval markers and chemical adhesion tests.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional pairing between geometric architectural strength (**Montserrat**) and micro-level mechanical legibility (**Inter**).

- **Headlines & Structural Titles (Montserrat):** Rendered in weights 700 through 800 with tight letter-spacing. Its geometric proportions mimic industrial signage, structural steel stamps, and technical brand framing.
- **Body & Numerical Data (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and rigorous. Calibrated for dense technical data, coverage tables, application ratios ($L/m^2$), and drying time benchmarks.
- **Labels, Badges & Technical Indicators:** Set in `Inter` semi-bold or bold with deliberate positive tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) in all-caps or title-case to evoke ASTM standard stamps, DIN certifications, and membrane thickness ratings (mils/microns).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined, 12-column architectural grid built around structural modularity and technical documentation layouts.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 1280px max-width container, 48px (`3rem`) page margins, and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 32px margins, 20px gutters. Content reflows into 4-column balanced pairs for product spec comparison.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins, 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Technical comparison tables turn into horizontally swipeable cards or collapsible accordion stacks.

### Spacing Cadence
- Rhythms adhere strictly to an 8pt base grid with a 4pt micro-unit for form fields and tag badges.
- Technical specifications maintain compressed row heights with `space-sm` (8px) internal padding to enable fast scanning of chemical and physical data points.
- Hero and structural module divisions utilize expansive `space-xl` (40px) or double-scale (80px) vertical margins to evoke industrial scale and spacious civil engineering builds.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy, atmospheric, or colorful drop shadows in favor of **engineered planar layering and calibrated architectural borders**. Depth represents tangible structural tiers—analogous to a multi-layer liquid membrane assembly (Primer -> Base Coat -> Reinforcement Mesh -> Top Coat).

### Depth Strategies
- **Layer 0 (Substrate/Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` base surface representing bare concrete or treated deck.
- **Layer 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`. Elevation is conveyed through a razor-thin technical shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Calculators):** Used for interactive surface area calculators, dropdown selectors, and active product tabs. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Spec Sheets & Material Overlays):** Used for technical sheet PDF previews and emergency leak diagnostics. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.04)`.
- **Blueprint Line Art & Accent Trims:** Instead of shadow elevation, select high-priority elements use a top 3px solid color line (e.g., `#F4B223` for warranty cards, `#C9181E` for urgent technical advisories).

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled **Soft (`1`)** shape language (4px base radii). 

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Core Elements:** Buttons, form inputs, badge enclosures, and table headers feature strict `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radiuses. This reflects precision-molded polymers, factory packaging, and laser-cut construction standards.
- **Structural Containers:** Larger product spec cards, quotation modules, and media wrappers scale up modestly to `8px` (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem`). Circular or pill shapes are avoided except for status pill indicator dots.
- **Hexagonal Accents:** The brand mark's faceted apex can be echoed in bullet icons, step-sequence numbers, and certified applicator seal frames, reinforcing the structural roof/membrane apex.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion / Cotización):** High-contrast Signal Red (`#C9181E`) background, crisp white label, 4px corner radius, bold `Inter` tracking. On hover: Darken to `#A81318` with slight vertical offset (`-1px`).
- **Secondary Action (Technical Specs & Documentation):** Solid Deep Industrial Blue (`#1B3B6F`), white text. Focus state rings with a 2px offset in `#F4B223`.
- **Outline / Specification Button:** 1.5px border in `#1B3B6F`, text `#1B3B6F`, transparent background. On hover: Fill `#1B3B6F` with text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** Ghost button with `#242A35` text, zero border, subtle `#F1F5F9` background on hover.

### Badges & Warranty Seals
- **Warranty Badges:** Solid `#F4B223` background with high-contrast `#1B3B6F` typography and an icon of the brand roof crest. Communicates terms like "10 AÑOS GARANTÍA DIRECTA".
- **Technical Parameter Chips:** Pale cool-slate fill (`#EEF2F6`), `#1B3B6F` text, 1px border `#CBD5E1`. Used for properties such as "Elongación > 400%", "Secado Rápido", "Resistente UV".
- **Certification Badges:** Clean white fill with a 1px `#C9181E` or `#1B3B6F` border, indicating ISO, ASTM, or VOC-compliant ratings.

### Technical Product Cards
- Encapsulated in pure `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and a top accent stroke denoting membrane family (e.g., Polyurethane = `#1B3B6F`, Acrylic Elastomeric = `#F4B223`, Rapid Leak Plug = `#C9181E`).
- Card layout: Product photo against a high-contrast industrial background, technical specification summary table (Rendimiento, Capas recomendadas, Tiempo de curado), and dual CTA actions (Primary "Solicitar Ficha", Secondary "Cotizar").

### Input Fields & Calculation Sliders
- **Inputs:** Height 44px, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, font `Inter` 15px.
- **Active / Focus:** 1.5px border `#1B3B6F` with an external glow ring of `rgba(27, 59, 111, 0.15)`.
- **Surface Area Calculator:** Slider track filled with `#1B3B6F`, thumb styled in `#F4B223` with a crisp 2px white outline. Dynamically outputs total liters and required primer cans in bold `Montserrat` numbers.

### Lists & Technical Data Tables
- Striped alternating table rows (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with subtle `#E2E8F0` dividers.
- Column headers set in uppercase `label-md` with `#1B3B6F` font color and a 2px `#1B3B6F` bottom rule.
- Checklists feature custom checkmarks rendered in `#1B3B6F` or `#F4B223` inside a micro square rather than soft organic circles.
